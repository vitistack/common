The NetworkNamespace conversion webhook (v1alpha1 <-> v1alpha2) has been deployed:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} rollout status deploy/{{ include "vitistack-crds.webhook.name" . }}

NOTE: The webhook requires cert-manager (with cainjector) in the cluster. Until
the webhook pods are Ready and the CA bundle has been injected, NetworkNamespace
operations will fail. Check readiness with:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get pods -l app.kubernetes.io/component=conversion-webhook

If you do not run cert-manager, reinstall with the webhook disabled to get CRDs only:

  --set conversionWebhook.enabled=false
